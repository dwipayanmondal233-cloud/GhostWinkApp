Install

  1. Tap DOWNLOAD APK and open the file. Allow Install unknown apps if asked.
  2. If Play Protect blocks it, see the fix below.
  3. Open GhostWink, tap START, and allow camera and notifications.
  4. Tap ALLOW STATUS DOT and switch it on (optional, but helpful).
  5. Tap ENABLE ACTIONS, open GhostWink, and turn it On.
  6. Tap CALIBRATE and follow the voice. Then practice typing SOS.

Problems and fixes

App blocked by Play Protect

Accessibility apps are flagged as sensitive. GhostWink uses it only to type and perform actions.

  1. Open Play Store, profile picture, Play Protect.
  2. Tap the gear and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Install the app, then turn scanning back on.
Accessibility switch is greyed out (Restricted setting)
  1. Phone Settings, Apps, GhostWink.
  2. Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Allow restricted settings.
  3. Go back to Accessibility and turn GhostWink on.

No ⋮ menu? Open Accessibility and tap GhostWink once, then return to App info.

Camera permission popup does not appear

Android blocks permission popups while another app draws over the screen. Stop other floating apps (bubbles, recorders, blue-light filters), then try again. Or grant it manually: Settings, Apps, GhostWink, Permissions, Camera, Allow while using the app.

Blinks are not detected

Tap CALIBRATE and follow the voice. Face the camera in good light, about an arm's length away. Take off glasses if they reflect light. You can also lower the Blink threshold slider.

Dots and dashes are mixed up

Move Dash starts at. If a quick blink becomes a dash, raise it. If a long blink becomes a dot, lower it. Keep a steady rhythm.

Letters are not typed into other apps

Check that GhostWink is on in Accessibility and that you are in TYPE mode. Tap the text box so the keyboard is open before you blink. After updating the app, turn the Accessibility switch off and on. Password fields are blocked on purpose.

It stops when the screen turns off

Most Android phones stop camera access when the screen is off, so GhostWink needs the screen on. Lower the screen brightness instead. Also set Settings, Apps, GhostWink, Battery, to Unrestricted.

I hear no voice

Turn up the media volume and make sure VOICE is ON in the app. In phone Settings, search "Text-to-speech" and select Speech Recognition and Synthesis from Google.

Mode keeps flipping by accident

You are closing your eyes for about 3 seconds. Blink shorter, or look away from the camera during long pauses.

"App not installed" when updating

Builds are signed with a debug key that changes between builds. Uninstall the old GhostWink first, then install the new one.

Safety

GhostWink is for convenience and accessibility. Do not rely on it for emergencies, driving, or anything critical. It never types into password fields. It has no internet permission, so nothing leaves your phone.
