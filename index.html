Practice mode

Open the app, tap PRACTICE MODE and follow 11 steps: E, T, S, O, SOS, enter CONTROL, scroll, back, start CURSOR, click a target, and return to TYPE. The app tells you what it saw if you get one wrong.

Install

  1. Tap DOWNLOAD APK and open the file. Allow Install unknown apps if asked.
  2. If Play Protect blocks it, see the fix below.
  3. Open GhostWink, tap START, and allow camera and notifications.
  4. Tap ALLOW STATUS DOT and switch it on. The monkey needs this too.
  5. Tap ENABLE ACTIONS, open GhostWink, and turn it On.
  6. Tap CALIBRATE, then open PRACTICE MODE.

Problems and fixes

App blocked by Play Protect

Accessibility apps are flagged as sensitive. GhostWink uses it only to type and perform actions.

  1. Open Play Store, profile picture, Play Protect.
  2. Tap the gear and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Install the app, then turn scanning back on.
Accessibility switch is greyed out
  1. Phone Settings, Apps, GhostWink.
  2. Tap ⋮, then Allow restricted settings.
  3. Return to Accessibility and turn GhostWink on.

No ⋮ menu? Open Accessibility and tap GhostWink once, then return to App info.

Camera permission popup does not appear

Android blocks permission popups while another app draws over the screen. Stop other floating apps, then retry. Or go to Settings, Apps, GhostWink, Permissions, Camera, Allow while using the app.

The monkey does not appear

You need CONTROL mode first (close eyes ~3 sec), then raise your eyebrows. Also allow Display over other apps and turn on ENABLE ACTIONS. The screen text shows which one is missing.

The monkey goes the wrong way or is too jumpy

Use FLIP LEFT/RIGHT in the app. Raise CURSOR SPEED if you must move your head too much, and lower it if it is jumpy. Sit in a steady position.

Blinks are not detected

Tap CALIBRATE. Face the camera in good light at arm's length. Take off glasses if they reflect light. You can also lower the Blink threshold.

Dots and dashes are mixed up

Move Dash starts at. If a quick blink becomes a dash, raise it. If a long blink becomes a dot, lower it.

Letters are not typed into other apps

Check that GhostWink is on in Accessibility and that you are in TYPE mode. Open the keyboard before blinking. After an update, turn the Accessibility switch off and on. Password fields are blocked on purpose.

It stops when the screen turns off

Most phones stop camera access with the screen off. Lower the brightness instead, and set Settings, Apps, GhostWink, Battery, to Unrestricted.

I hear no voice

Turn up media volume and check VOICE is ON. In phone Settings, search "Text-to-speech" and choose Speech Recognition and Synthesis from Google.

"App not installed" when updating

Debug builds are signed with a key that changes between builds. Uninstall the old GhostWink first, then install the new one.

Safety

GhostWink is for convenience and accessibility. Do not rely on it for emergencies, driving, or anything critical. It never types into password fields and has no internet permission.
